FAQ

Questions, answered

Is each institute’s data private?

Yes. All student, class, payment, report, and settings queries are scoped to the authenticated institute on the server.

Can I collect fees in installments?

Yes. Record any number of partial payments; balances and fee status update automatically.

How does yearly payment work?

The server creates and verifies a Razorpay order before activating access. Frontend payment messages are never trusted.

Can I export my records?

Reports can be filtered by date and exported to CSV. Student fee receipts download as PDF.
